[h1]Holding Manager (RUSBAR Edition)[/h1]

[b]Version:[/b] 1.0.0
[b]For Crusader Kings III:[/b] 1.19.0.6
[b]Languages:[/b] English, Russian, French, German, Spanish, Korean, Simplified Chinese

Holding Manager (RUSBAR Edition) is a vanilla-focused, stabilized fork of Holding Manager Continued 1.16. It keeps the convenient holding-management interface while rebuilding its internals for the current vanilla game.

[h2]Features[/h2]

[list]
[*]A compact control panel integrated into the county view.
[*]Immediate construction, upgrade, replacement, and removal of supported regular buildings.
[*]Configurable extra building slots for the player and AI.
[*]Conversion between supported holding types, with optional Nomad and Temple Citadel buttons.
[*]Feudalization of eligible personal or vassal holdings according to game rules.
[*]Personal-domain Build All, Upgrade All, and Clear All decisions.
[*]Realm-wide Build All, Upgrade All, and Clear All decisions when vassal management is enabled.
[*]Exact previews for all six mass decisions, grouped by county and holding: you can see every building that will be constructed, upgraded, or removed before confirming.
[*]Configurable prices, instant-construction surcharge, cooldowns, action scope, available buttons, slot limits, and AI behavior.
[*]Gold, prestige, and treasury costs calculated consistently for personal and vassal holdings.
[*]Show/Hide decisions for keeping the decision list tidy.
[/list]

[h2]Why RUSBAR Edition?[/h2]

Compared with Holding Manager Continued, this edition prioritizes maximum compatibility with the current vanilla mechanics instead of preserving outdated compatibility layers.

[list]
[*]The county and title windows were rebased on the supported vanilla GUI.
[*]Building eligibility was rebuilt from current vanilla definitions.
[*]Innovations, culture parameters, government, terrain, holding type, prerequisite chains, county limits, and other vanilla restrictions are respected.
[*]Cultural exceptions that unlock buildings earlier than the ordinary innovation path are preserved.
[*]Construction, upgrades, slots, costs, and mass actions now behave consistently in vassal holdings.
[*]Mass construction can add permitted slots to vassal holdings just as it does in the personal domain.
[*]Numerous inherited script errors, invalid references, typos, localization problems, encoding issues, and incorrect calculations were fixed.
[*]Old City Walls/COW code, obsolete compatibility branches, duplicate data, dead rules, and unused interface copies were removed.
[*]Fork-owned files and objects use the HM_RE_ namespace to reduce collisions.
[*]Vanilla building requirements can be reproducibly synchronized after CK3 updates.
[/list]

Duchy buildings are intentionally excluded from mass-building catalogs in version 1.0.0.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]

The base mod targets the unmodified vanilla game. Other building mods require explicit catalog support through a separate compatibility patch.

HM_RE replaces:
[list]
[*]gui/window_county_view.gui
[*]gui/window_title.gui
[/list]

Mods replacing either file require a GUI compatibility patch. Place such a patch after both mods.
